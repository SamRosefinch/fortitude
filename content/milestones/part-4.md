# Milestones & Benchmarks (Part IV)

Intermediate volume thresholds and combined movement capacity.

- [ ] Accumulate 30 total strict Full Push-Ups within a single workout session
- [ ] Complete 3 sets of 15 unbroken Bodyweight Squats
- [ ] Perform 3 sets of 12 Australian Rows with strict form
- [ ] Complete 12 Single-Leg Bridges per leg consecutively
- [ ] Accumulate 60 total seconds of Active Dead Hang across multiple sets

## Progression Log 04

Higher rep density enhances muscular endurance and prepares connective tissues for heavy resistance.
