Calisthenics Quick Reference Guide

Target: Beginner progression focusing on structural movement mastery, milestone completion, and tiered difficulty challenges.

Goal Objective: Build lean muscle mass, systemic strength capacity, achieve structured milestones, and conquer graded challenges ranging from Easy to Grandmaster.

System Architecture

This reference framework relies on biomechanical loading gradients to progressively build joint resilience and structural muscle adaptation.

Push-Up Progression Matrix

Tier 1
Incline Push-Up
3 × 10–12 Reps

Place hands on an elevated stable surface, maintaining a straight body line.

Tier 2
Knee Push-Up
3 × 8–10 Reps

Kneeling plank position with rigid core tension while pressing torso.

Tier 3
Full Push-Up
3 × Max Reps

Full rigid plank execution with elbows at a 90-degree angle or lower.

Technical Analysis

Graduated loading vectors systematically scale load from incline to standard body weight, ensuring safe structural adaptation.

Squat Progression Matrix

Tier 1
Box / Chair Squat
3 × 12–15 Reps

Lower hips back and down until seated briefly on a chair, then stand up.

Tier 2
Bodyweight Squat
3 × 12–15 Reps

Full depth unassisted squat with heels flat and torso upright.

Tier 3
Paused Squat
3 × 8–10 Reps

Hold a 2-second pause at the bottom deep position before driving upward.

Technical Analysis

Develops lower limb power and composition across quadriceps and glutes while maintaining vertical shin alignment.

Posterior Chain Progression Matrix

Tier 1
Short-Lever Bridge
3 × 15 Reps

Lie on back with knees bent, feet close to hips. Drive through heels.

Tier 2
Standard Glute Bridge
3 × 12–15 Reps

Feet extended further out to target glutes and hamstring recruitment.

Tier 3
Single-Leg Bridge
3 × 8–10 Reps / Leg

Lift one leg off the floor and drive entirely through the planted heel.

Technical Analysis

Reinforces pelvic stability and posterior chain activation to counter anterior dominance.

Vertical Pull Progression Matrix

Tier 1
Active Dead Hang
3 × 20–30s Hold

Hang from a bar with shoulders engaged and scapulae depressed.

Tier 2
Australian Row
3 × 8–10 Reps

Pull chest to a low bar or table edge while maintaining a rigid body line.

Tier 3
Negative Pull-Up
3 × 3–5 Reps

Jump to top position and lower yourself down on a 5-second count.

Technical Analysis

Builds essential back thickness and scapular control, balancing the structural pull requirements.

Core Stability Progression Matrix

Tier 1
Kneeling Plank
3 × 30s Hold

Maintain a straight line from knees to shoulders with braced abs.

Tier 2
Standard Plank
3 × 30–45s Hold

Full forearm plank with zero spinal sag or lumbar hyperextension.

Tier 3
Long-Lever Plank
3 × 20–30s Hold

Elbows shifted forward past shoulders to dramatically increase load.

Technical Analysis

Trains anti-extension endurance of the transverse abdominis, transferring stability into upper and lower body patterns.

Execution Parameters

  • Frequency: Perform routines 3 times per week with 48 hours recovery between sessions.
  • Time Under Tension: Execute a 2-second eccentric phase (lowering) followed by a strong concentric phase.
  • Progressive Overload: Advance tiers only when clean execution of current tier rep ranges is mastered.

Physiological Recovery

Protein synthesis windows and neuromuscular adaptations peak during recovery blocks.
